Rules, not AI

Crime is checked first. A suspect is never asked a question.

Three questions, always in this order. Once there is suspicion the SOP forbids asking the customer anything, so suspicion comes before any request. Closing has the strictest test, because a wrongful close is the costliest mistake.

Rules, not AI

A letter on file proves nothing. Its facts have to match.

A document explains a payment only when it states the amount to the penny, names the party and is dated before the money moved. Then the alert is tested again without that money.

Rules, not AI

No model in the loop. The same answer every run.

Plain rules read typed facts: amounts, dates, accounts, names. Every decision traces to a line of code and a fact in the file, and the reason is written only from the facts the check used.
